# Scope

The customer journey from initial research through to approaching a comparison site, broker or insurer.

Consumers who are already using LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot to research, understand or compare insurance. Solutions that could improve the quality of information, questions, understanding or preparation before the consumer reaches an insurance professional or comparison site.

The three issues identified in the Research Sprint: the quality of information varies depending on how consumers use and prompt LLMs; LLMs may not have access to accurate, current or UK-specific insurance information; consumers can arrive at insurers or comparison sites with assumptions already shaped by AI.

Teams can choose which part of this problem they want to tackle. They might focus on the consumer interaction with the LLM, improving or validating the information available, helping consumers identify gaps or misunderstandings, or supporting the handover into the regulated insurance journey. High-level consumer journey shown below.

**Life event or trigger** → **Review existing position** → **Research** → **Compare** → **Evaluate** → **Decide** → **Price & Terms** → **Purchase** → **Qualification Period** → **Re-evaluate** → **Manage / reassess** → **claim**

# Constraints

Work with the LLMs consumers already use, rather than relying on people moving to an entirely new AI platform.

Recognise that LLM outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or based on information from other markets/countries.

Avoid relying on access to confidential or proprietary insurer data that would not realistically be available.

Protect consumer privacy. Full AI conversations should not automatically be shared with insurers or comparison platforms, although consumer-approved summaries or information could be shared.

Recognise the boundaries around regulated financial advice. Any solution moving into regulated advice would need to meet the appropriate requirements.
